one more thing to reason: 
the implication of two selves (or rather two parts of one self)

the Still (wisest but not too outspoken)
the Loud (the one in control of talking and relating to others)

the Still is hard to find and navigate to but is the most resourceful, its wise, its hidden but valuable
its in charge of epiphanies and understanding
its within and pure and true 
from which creativity comes from
once or twice it seizes the body from the doer when given enough space to go nuts 
it cant be forced to act 
pressure is futile

but using it makes you have the privilege of using it more

but the Loud
it is the default mode, handles conversations and is sufficient for a normal life but to be extraordinary you need to find the listener

so in all 
we are God and we make our own realities
everything is subjective
every experience, feeling, knowledge is unique to you

you have to learn to chew to be better 
prompt the listener more and see wonders
